# Ragaflow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 21, 2026_

Ragaflow ("we", "us") makes an app and website that stream Indian classical
music sessions for focus, sleep, and meditation. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain because the
short version is the honest version: we collect the minimum needed to run the
app, and we don't sell your data.

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email
address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider,
Supabase). If you set a display name, we store that too.

**Your activity in the app.** To make the app work and remember your
preferences, we store:

- Tracks and ragas you favorite
- Your default track for each moment (focus, sleep, meditate)
- Listening sessions — which track, which moment, when it started and ended, and
  how long you listened
- Certifications — cumulative listening time per ragam, used to show your
  progress

**Analytics events.** We record basic product events (for example: app opened, a
moment was viewed, an error occurred) to understand how the app is used and to
fix problems. Each event includes an anonymous session identifier, the event
type, and the platform (web or iOS). When you are signed in, events may be
associated with your account.

**We do not collect** your location, contacts, photos, health data, or device
advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app
tracking SDKs, and Ragaflow does not track you across other companies' apps or
websites.

## How we use it

- To authenticate you and keep you signed in
- To save your favorites, defaults, and listening progress across your devices
- To operate and improve the app, and to diagnose errors
- To communicate with you about your account or the service when necessary

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data
brokers or advertisers.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored in our backend provider, **Supabase**, and audio is
delivered through our content-delivery provider, **Cloudflare**. These providers
process data on our behalf to run the service. Data may be stored and processed
in the United States.

## Your choices

- **Access or correct your data:** update your profile in the app, or contact us.
- **Delete your account:** contact us at the email below and we will delete your
  account and associated personal data.
- **Stop analytics:** analytics is tied to using the app; if you would prefer we
  not associate events with your account, contact us.

## Children

Ragaflow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the date above and,
where appropriate, notify you in the app.

## Contact

Questions, requests, or account deletion: **support@ragaflow.io**
